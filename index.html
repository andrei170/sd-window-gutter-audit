Finding 1 — Commercial Buyers Do Not Ring Mobiles
This is the gap between the work you have and the work you want
Forms anywhere on the site 0 Form input fields 0 Email address published none found Tap-to-call number 07847 296805 — works

Your phone link is right, and for a domestic customer that is enough. Commercial is a different buyer entirely. A facilities manager, a managing agent or a site manager needs to send you something in writing — a site list, a spec, a request for a quote — usually because he has to show his own boss he got three prices.

He cannot do that from your website. No form, no email address. So either he rings a mobile and hopes you are not up a ladder, or he goes to a firm whose site lets him send the job over at four in the afternoon and get on with his day.

The two to three extra jobs a week you said you could take are most likely sitting in exactly that gap.

Finding 2 — Nothing Is Measured
Read from your served page source
Google Analytics (GA4) ✗ not present Google Tag Manager ✗ not present Ads conversion tracking ✗ not present Meta pixel ✗ not present

No visit, call or enquiry is counted anywhere. Which means nobody can tell you which of your commercial work came from the website, from word of mouth, or from a van seen on a job.

This is the first thing we would fix and the cheapest. It has to exist before any advertising is worth running, because Google's bidding optimises against conversion data and with none it optimises for clicks — and clicks are not contracts.

Finding 3 — You Are Hiring, And Your Site Does Not Say So
You told our team you want to bring on one to two more people

There is no careers page, no vacancy and no way to apply anywhere on the site. In window and gutter cleaning the person you want is usually already working for a competitor, and the moment he looks you up is the moment you either capture him or do not.

Adding a short “join the team” page with the pay, the hours and a two-minute form costs nothing and works while you are on a job. It is the same fix as the commercial form, pointed at a different person.

What Is Genuinely Good
You are ahead of most of the trade on these
  • Your title tag is properly written. “SD Window & Gutter Cleaning | Trusted Local Team | Book Today” — service, positioning and a call to action. Most sites we audit have nothing like this.
  • Tap-to-call works on mobile, first tap.
  • A commercial book of 8–10 jobs a week is the hard part, and you already have it. Commercial is repeat, contracted and far more valuable than domestic.
  • A team of three — you are past the point where the business depends entirely on one pair of hands.

This is not a broken business with a website problem. It is a working commercial operation whose website cannot take an enquiry or an application.
